## Interpretation and limits

This completes C1 because the real receipt supplements the already passing
native invariant negatives, journal/effects equality, and transparent-oracle and
Halo2 differentials. It does not activate a verifier, make the system safe for
real funds, or complete C4, C6, C7, or A5.

C4 remains open until the large receipt is published as a versioned release
artifact with canonical public inputs, negative cases, proof-size bounds, and a
portable offline-verification package. A macOS M1 compile check of the verifier
with guest rebuilding disabled passed. The full M1 receipt verification was
stopped at the user's request when ending the paid session; this does not replace
or invalidate the two successful verifications on the evidence host.

RISC Zero 3.0.6 includes absolute Rust source paths in guest panic metadata.
Therefore the reviewed image ID above is tied to the canonical
`/workspace/vault` build root even though the source and lockfiles were
unchanged. Offline verification must use the reviewed digest rather than
silently substituting a guest rebuilt at another checkout path.
